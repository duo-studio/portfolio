Homepage Concept Annotations
Le Mondo · Hero + Immediate Module
This concept keeps Claude's clean multi-frame presentation, but the design language itself stays specific to Le Mondo's poster, cutout, artist-run energy.
Before

Join the Club leads

The current homepage opens with a membership pitch and general identity language. Visitors have to scroll or click before they see a real show with a date attached to it.

After

Programming comes first

The first read is now a specific event, with a visible date, time, and ticket path. Membership still exists, but it follows the calendar instead of blocking it.

1
Featured event replaces the pitch
DIGITAL XSCAPE becomes the hero, so a first-time visitor can tell what is happening this week in a single glance.
Programming visibility
2
The next 3 shows stay visible
Lockbox, BATH SALLTS, and Embodied Acting sit beside or directly below the hero so the site communicates volume, not just one-off promotion.
Perceived programming volume
3
Identity and membership move down
The artist-owned venue description and JOIN THE CLUB language still have a place, but they now follow the reason to care.
Hierarchy correction
4
The collage language stays intact
Paper tones, loud color chips, taped posters, and imperfect rotation keep the concept rooted in Le Mondo instead of smoothing it into a generic events template.
Brand preservation
What happens when an artist-run venue behaves like a venue on the homepage?
