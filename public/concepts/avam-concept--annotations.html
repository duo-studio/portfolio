Duo Studio concept notes

AVAM can be louder and clearer at the same time.

This concept keeps the museum's joy, eccentricity, aqua header, red/yellow CTAs, big art, and quote-driven voice, then gives the first screen more practical direction: buy tickets, see what is on, find events, or plan a visit.

Come see what imagination can do.

The redesigned first view still feels like AVAM: strange, colorful, playful, and handmade. The difference is that the homepage now gives visitors a useful path before asking them to wander.

Tickets Primary action stays loud and obvious.
On View The current mega-exhibition anchors the hero.
Events Kinetic Sculpture Race and near-term events move higher.
Visit Hours, admission, and address are visible immediately.
1

Brand weirdness stays intact.

The concept does not sanitize AVAM. It keeps the candy-colored palette, all-caps nav, playful buttons, italic quotes, real artwork, and a little intentional tilt so the page still feels handmade and alive.

2

The first screen works harder.

The current homepage leads with a beautiful full-width image, but practical choices sit lower. This version keeps a big art moment while adding tickets, hours, admission, location, and audience paths above the fold.

3

Programming becomes visible immediately.

Fantastic Realities anchors the hero, while Kinetic Sculpture Race and the next events sit nearby. Visitors can understand what is on now and what is coming without starting from the Events page.

4

The joy has a job.

The decorative energy is also functional: colorful cards separate visit planning, exhibitions, events, and rentals, giving the visitor clear doors into AVAM's wonderfully crowded universe.

Above the fold: art, action, and wayfinding.

The concept treats AVAM's homepage like a cabinet of curiosities with labels on the drawers. It invites discovery, but does not make visitors guess where to begin.
