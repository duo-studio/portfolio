Duo Studio concept notes

BMI does not need a louder homepage. It needs a calmer decision path.

This pass keeps the BMI-native visual direction, then reduces how much the page asks visitors to process at once. The homepage becomes a sequence: orient, choose a path, confirm practical details, then learn more.

Work matters. See how Baltimore was built.

The concept now presents BMI like a live public homepage: a museum visit, a school trip, a waterfront event, and a civic story all have a clear starting point.

Visit Hours, tickets, parking, and the address are easy to confirm.
Education Teachers can quickly find field trips and classroom programs.
Host an Event The waterfront venue path is visible without crowding the hero.
Support BMI Membership and giving connect back to Baltimore civic memory.
1

Closer to BMI's existing brand feel.

The current BMI site is light, direct, and institutional: blue headings, orange CTAs, white space, rounded photography, and dashed blue rules. The redesign now extends that language instead of importing a more expressive Le Mondo-style system.

2

Each zone has one job.

The hero invites a museum visit. The choice bar routes audiences. The facts strip confirms logistics. Proof points come later, so visitors are not asked to read every audience case at once.

3

Audience paths become choices, not content blocks.

Visit, Education, Host an Event, and Support are still visible near the top, but they are now compact links. The fuller explanation waits until someone selects a path or scrolls with intent.

4

Proof is summarized before it expands.

Teacher ratings, waterfront rentals, and historic building context are reduced to the strongest proof points. The page still sells BMI's breadth, but in a calmer rhythm.

The strategic shift stays the same.

The page is quieter, but the thesis remains: a working museum needs a working website. The UX pass makes that idea easier to digest by revealing information in layers.
